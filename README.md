### <img src="https://media.giphy.com/media/hvRJCLFzcasrR4ia7z/giphy.gif" width="28px"> &nbsp;About me

Backend engineer at **Superfone**, working on a telephony + CRM + WhatsApp
platform with AI voice agents on top. Day to day that means a large
**Node.js / TypeScript** backend and a **Python** real-time voice service —
plus the four services between a phone call and a language model.

```yaml
location:  Bengaluru, India  (from Faridabad, Haryana)
role:      Backend engineer — AI, messaging & billing surfaces
stack:     TypeScript · Node · Python · PostgreSQL · Redis · Kafka
into:      distributed systems, observability, telephony, LLM plumbing
offline:   music, and being marginally better than yesterday
```

- 🎙️ **Real-time voice AI** — audio over WebSockets, `VAD → STT → LLM → TTS`,
  multi-provider abstractions with failover, and the latency budget that keeps
  a conversation feeling like one.
- 💬 **WhatsApp Business Platform** — chatbot flow engine, AI auto-reply,
  campaigns, and an event-driven lifecycle-messaging system with idempotent,
  cancellable jobs.
- 💸 **Billing & entitlements** — wallets, subscriptions, metered usage, and the
  reconciliation work that finds revenue leaking through the gaps.
- 📈 **Observability** — a Slack alerting stack I ported across two languages,
  dashboards over call telemetry, and a real fondness for RCAs.
